# How it works today — Northwind Labs demand reporting

One person does each step and hands off to the next. Nothing runs end to end.

1. **Pull the data** — an analyst exports the campaign and touchpoint data into a spreadsheet. ~2h/week.
2. **Group the sources into channels** — the analyst hand-sorts the raw source strings into channel buckets. New/odd values are judged case by case. ~1.5h/week.
3. **Build the weekly report** — demand gen assembles the MQL and channel-mix report from the sheet. ~1h/week.
4. **Sales tally** — the SDR team keeps its own count of accepted leads in a separate sheet. ~1h/week.
5. **Definition tweaks** — thresholds and groupings are adjusted ad hoc when they seem off. Week 7: engagement scoring recalibrated to better reflect intent (routine update).
